Responsible AI & Clinical Boundaries

MindCare AI is an educational technology demonstration. Machine learning models identify statistical correlations within survey training data; they do not possess clinical diagnostic authority, medical judgment, or psychological intuition.

Not a Diagnosis: Predictions must never be interpreted as clinical diagnoses of depression, anxiety, or other mental disorders.
Professional Care First: If you feel overwhelmed, anxious, or distressed, please consult a certified mental health professional immediately.
