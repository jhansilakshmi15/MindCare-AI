Sleep Hygiene

Adequate sleep restores cognitive function and regulates emotional reactivity. Disrupted sleep compounds stress significantly.

  • Maintain a consistent sleep-wake schedule, even on weekends.
  • Avoid mobile and laptop screens 45 minutes before sleeping.
  • Keep the room cool, dim, and quiet for deeper REM sleep.
Breathwork & Grounding

Controlled respiration activates the parasympathetic nervous system, lowering cortisol and heart rate within minutes.

Box Breathing Technique (4-4-4-4):
Inhale (4s) → Hold (4s) → Exhale (4s) → Hold empty (4s). Repeat 4 cycles.
  • Practice the 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding exercise during acute anxiety.
  • Take 3 deep physiological sighs (two quick inhales, slow exhale).
Physical Activity & Movement

Physical movement stimulates neurogenesis and releases endorphins that counteract chronic study fatigue.

  • Aim for 20-30 minutes of moderate aerobic activity daily (brisk walk, cycling).
  • Stand up and stretch every 60 minutes during long coding/study sessions.
  • Get 10 minutes of direct morning sunlight to reset your circadian clock.
Social Connections

Isolation magnifies perceived difficulties. Meaningful interpersonal sharing creates psychological buffer zones.

  • Schedule regular coffee breaks with trusted friends or peer group.
  • Talk openly about engineering deadlines without competitive pressure.
  • Engage in collaborative study circles rather than isolating when stuck.
Workload & Exam Pressure

Proactive project breakdown prevents deadline panic and mental exhaustion.

  • Use the Pomodoro framework (25 mins focused work, 5 mins true rest).
  • Decompose massive final-year project deliverables into daily milestones.
  • Set clear evening boundaries: stop answering work emails past 9:00 PM.
Professional Psychological Care

Seeking therapy is a proactive, strength-based decision, comparable to seeing a doctor for physical injuries.

  • University counseling cells provide free, confidential student guidance.
  • Therapy equips you with personalized Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) tools.
  • Early consultation prevents mild stress from escalating into chronic burnout.
